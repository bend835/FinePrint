Why Is It Important?

Terms and conditions are important for businesses as it informs the user about the business's practices and relation to the user. However, due to the length and number of forms people have to sign, most people wind up skipping the terms and conditions. This can lead to users accidentally signing up for something they wouldn't agree to had they read them. While an extreme example, we can see the effects of this from the unfortunate passing of Dr. Kanokporn Tangsuan at Disney World, Florida. In 2023, Jeffery Piccolo and his wife Dr. Kanokporn Tangsuan were eating at a restaurant in the Disney World resort, when Dr. Kanokporn Tangsuan ingested food containing allergens and died due to the severe allergic reaction. After this incident, Jeffery Piccolo filed a wrongful death lawsuit against Disney and the owners of the restaurant. Later, Disney filed court documents in May saying the lawsuit should be dismissed and resolved because of the terms Piccolo agreed to when he signed up for a free trial of Disney+. Since then Disney has dropped this claim, but it still shows how much information is kept in these terms and conditions and how important it is to review them. That is why our website is important. Reading all those documents just isn't possible if you aren't familiar with the legal terms. Our website can help solve this issue by summarizing the documents so you get all the necessary information without spending multiple hours trying to understand the legal jargon.
